[Verse 1 — cold open, no intro; clipped, technical, controlled]
They never banned the animal. They banned the trade — the buying, the selling, the having it sold to you.
Left the management exemptions standing, because the herd doesn't read the legislation
and something still has to keep the numbers off the young trees.
So I stopped being a customer. Took the coaching, sat the written, sat the practical,
put my name on a register that anyone can search, next to my address.
Deer Management Level Two. There's a certificate. Ruth framed it, which I think was a joke.

[Hook — chanted, stacked]
They never made it illegal to eat. They made it illegal to be sold.
So I am not a man who buys meat. I am a man who is licensed to manage a herd.
Every step of it is lawful and I can show you the paperwork for all of it.
Nobody in this county is doing anything wrong, least of all me.
I'd sit the exam again in the morning. I'd sit it in the rain.

[Verse 2 — same pulse, tighter]
Cull season: an hour before light, high seat on the north edge, wind in my face and the rifle cold.
You take the old and the sick and the ones the count says the ground can't carry.
The plan says which ones. I don't get to have a favourite. I file the return every quarter and they have never queried it.
Field dressing is the part nobody puts in the brochure. It's not difficult. It is not nothing, either.
Larder, hang it seven days, break it down on the bench in the garage.
The rest of the county gets the same shot bird as everyone else. I get the haunch.

[The wink — spoken, flat, cold]
Ruth's been vegetarian since before we met — twenty-six years, and she has never made a
thing of it. She signed the witness section on my application without reading
past the first page. The garage freezer is mine. I do it on the nights she's on
shift, and I open the back door so the house doesn't smell of it when she's in.

[Final hook — chanted, weightier, full band]
They never made it illegal to eat. They made it illegal to be sold.
So I am not a man who buys meat. I am a man who is licensed to manage a herd.
I COOK IT ON HER NIGHTS. I OPEN THE BACK DOOR. SHE FRAMED THE CERTIFICATE.
Every step of it is lawful and I can show you the paperwork for all of it.
I'd sit the exam again in the morning. I'd sit it in the rain.
