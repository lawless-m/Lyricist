[Intro — cold, mechanized pulse, spoken low]
Yield, in this trade, means what comes back out heavier than it went in.
My father used the word the other way round. He meant giving in.

[Hook — chanted, stacked]
Every word on that pack is true, and I wrote half of them myself.
A hundred kilos in, a hundred and twenty-eight back out —
and the difference is water I taught the meat to hold.
You haven't got E451 in your cupboard. That's what you're paying me for.
Nobody's been lied to. Nobody's been fed. I'll set the same number Monday.

[The wink — spoken, flat, cold]
There's a bird in my own oven most Sundays. A proper one, from the farm shop at
Ashby, butter up under the skin, three hours, low. Then I drive a pack of ours
up to my father's and put it in his fridge. He was thirty-one years on the
block. He says it's the best chicken he's had in years.

[Verse 1 — clipped, technical, controlled]
Injector head, ninety-six pins, brine down at four degrees and moving.
The tripoly opens the protein, the protein takes the water, the water stays through the cook.
Tumble it under vacuum till the surface goes tacky and the drum changes note —
that part's on no spec sheet anywhere. That's standing next to it for twenty years.
Starch to stop it weeping in the pack. Dextrose so the outside browns like something roasted.
All of it declared, in order of weight, in the font the law says. I have never been out on a check.

[Verse 2 — rapped, escalation]
The supermarket sets the shelf price before the bird has hatched. I'm the one who makes that number work.
Two points of yield is a shift of fourteen keeping their hours and their overtime.
I could take it past thirty if they let me — but it goes glassy, and it sweats, and you'd see it through the film.
So I stop it exactly where it stops looking like water. Nobody taught me where that is.
That is the only thing I have ever been genuinely good at.
He can take a shoulder apart in under a minute. There's nobody left paying for that.
