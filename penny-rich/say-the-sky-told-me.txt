[Verse 1 — fast, banjo roll, boom-chuck guitar, fiddle answering the line ends. Bright warm female lead vocal.]
Whole row had their washing out on the Sunday and I never put a peg in.
Denise asked me why and I said I'd do mine Monday, and I left it there.
Come half past two it come down like a tap and it went on till dark.
Denise had a duvet cover out. A big one.

[Pre-chorus — mandolin chop tightens, bass pushes, harmony steps up behind]
The gate had gone tight on the Friday
and the gate goes tight before the rain

[Chorus — full band, close three-part harmony in from the second line]
So say the sky told me.
Say the sky told me, say I've got a feeling, say I'm lucky with a line of wash.
It isn't the sky. It's the gate. And I have said so, out loud, to people.
She's lucky with the weather.
She has always been lucky with the weather.

[Verse 2 — the working, fast and specific]
Gate swells. Drain smells. Swallows come down and go along the hedge instead of over it.
Cows on the Ridgeway lie down on the left of that field and not the right.
Kettle takes longer. Don't ask me why the kettle takes longer, but it does.
Four of them together and I'll bring the washing in. Three and I'll chance it.
That's not a feeling. That's four things, and I have been counting them since I was small.
Denise says I must have been a farmer in another life.
I was a farmer's girl in this one. She knows that. She's known me since school.

[Chorus — identical words, harmony wider]
So say the sky told me.
Say the sky told me, say I've got a feeling, say I'm lucky with a line of wash.
It isn't the sky. It's the gate. And I have said so, out loud, to people.
She's lucky with the weather.
She has always been lucky with the weather.

[The plain line and the break — band stops dead, one flat line, no vibrato, no repeat]
I've told them about the gate. I've told them all about the gate.

[Instrumental break — banjo, fiddle, mandolin trading eight bars each, fast and happy, and gone]

[Final chorus — biggest, full three-part close harmony all the way through, everybody singing the wrong version with her]
So say the sky told me.
Say the sky told me, say I've got a feeling, say I'm lucky with a line of wash.
It isn't the sky. It's the gate. And I have said so, out loud, to people.
She's lucky with the weather.
She'll always be lucky with the weather.

[Hard bluegrass tag — everybody hits the last note together and stops dead, no fade]
