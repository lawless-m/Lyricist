[Verse 1 — fast, clipped, dry, sampler stabs, both kits chattering. flat, under-reacting]
they sell it off the roll and the roll is stood up in the corner like a person
the tape goes round me and comes back and he writes down the bigger of the numbers
chalk on the hip, chalk on the shoulder, chalk comes off, that is what chalk is for
a bale in the back that used to be coats and is going to be coats
shoddy is a real word, it is a cloth, it is what you get when you pull a coat back into wool
brown paper, a length of string, and you do not get to see the middle
I have never seen the middle of a roll and I have been in this trade all my life

[Pre-hook — no chopping. the sampler stacks the tape measure and the drums thicken under it]
thirty-six — thirty-eight — forty — forty-two —
and it does not stop at me

[Hook — fast, chanted, gang backing]
run it out, chalk it, cut it wide, wrap it
feel the width
nobody unrolls it, they take the number off the end and that is the whole of the looking
forty-four. forty-four and a half.
feel the width
feel the width

[Verse 2 — clipped again, the stream thickens, the non-sequiturs get stranger]
mungo is harder than shoddy, mungo is what you get when the rag will not come apart nice
a woman on the market with a bolt across two trestles, hitting it, and it makes the sound
they weigh me in with the offcuts because the offcuts weigh the same
seconds bin, seconds bin, the flaw is in the middle and they roll it so the middle is inside
there is a line down me where the fold has been and they take the price off the line
a coat comes back in as rag and goes out as a coat and comes back in as rag
and the word for how fine the wool is, is quality. that is just the word for it.

[The dissolve — mock-composed, thinning, the sampler runs the tape over the vocal until the numbers are all that is left]
I can get you a straight run off this, no join, you would never find the join —
thirty-six — you would never — thirty-eight — never find —
forty — never — forty-two — nev—
(pitched down, looped, the room is only numbers)

[Final hook — biggest, whole room, mantra degraded, pitched and half-swallowed, ends on the tape left running]
run it out, chalk it, cut it wide, wrap it
nobody unrolls it, they take the number off the end
feel the width
feel the wid—
feel th—
(the tape running out, left running, no ending)
