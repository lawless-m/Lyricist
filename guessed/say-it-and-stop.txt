[Verse 1 — dry slowed breakbeat, Rhodes, vinyl crackle, voice close and thin and forward]
I read a thread about it. The whole thread. Every reply.
Somebody said: finish the sentence, and then stop.
Don't add the bit on the end. Don't do the little laugh.
I've read that forum since it started. There's nothing of mine on it.

[Hook — barely bigger, strings smear in, low end thickens]

say it and stop.
It worked. First go. Twenty years of doing the other thing.
how many is that, then. how many is that.

[Verse 2 — the incident, dated and small]
Wednesday. The eleven o'clock. I said the thing and I stopped.
And the room went quiet for a second and the quiet was mine.
Somebody said sorry, go on. So I went on.
Then I went back to my desk, and I didn't tell anyone it worked.

[Hook — same words, worse now]

say it and stop.
It worked. First go. Twenty years of doing the other thing.

[Wordless — flat hum over the loop, four bars, no words]

[The drop-out — everything cuts. no beat, no bass, no crackle, no reverb. one line, dry, alone]
I've written down what I did, in case it stops working.

[The loop returns unchanged, indifferent]

[Final hook — her own voice doubled and layered, second voice half a beat behind and drowned at the back of the hall. no other voices. ever.]
say it and stop.
say it and stop.
It worked. First go. And I have got it written down.
how many is that, then. how many is that.
