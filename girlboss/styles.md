Late-90s / early-00s indie hip-hop. Live-sounding breakbeat, filtered bass, and one cheap stab texture rotated per song — clavinet, Rhodes, dusty horn-section hits, scratched vinyl stabs, a chopped soul sample, wah guitar. (Organ was the default long enough to wear out; don't let any single texture become the new organ.) Female vocal, conversational-to-fast, close-mic'd, dry — the voice sits in front of the beat, not in it. Hook can be all-female-gang-chanted or doubled. No sung chorus. Nothing polished; the record should sound like it cost very little and knew exactly what it was doing.
