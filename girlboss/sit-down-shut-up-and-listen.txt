[Verse 1 — live-room breakbeat, filtered bass, cheap Rhodes stab; voice close-mic'd, dry, fast, pleased with itself]
Ten a.m., main stage, launch day — the full circus:
two thousand seats, a press pen, the board lined up along the back.
I built this thing from a spare bedroom: the app, the ad stack, the tone of voice.
Half this room has been shorting us since March. I had them leave the house lights on.

Comms said keep it corporate. Legal said soften slide twelve.
I kept the heels, I softened nothing, and I opened with the numbers —
because I'm good at this: I know every figure on every slide by heart,
and I know what a room full of doubters does when you take the microphone off the stand.

[Hook — all-female gang chant, flat, two lines only]
Sit down, shut up, and listen.
This won't take long.

[Verse 2 — same loop, bass up a notch; delivery stays deadpan]
Row two, aisle seat: the man whose fund has led the short,
flown in to watch me miss the guidance, printed list of questions on his lap.
So I walked the roadmap off the stage and gave the growth slide from the floor,
one hand on the arm of his chair. He stopped taking notes.

By the Q&A his list was folded down to the size of a receipt.
He didn't raise his hand. He raised his eyes, occasionally.
I announced the after-party from the stage — invitation only —
and looked at nobody in particular. He checked his lanyard like it might have changed.

[Hook — chant, unchanged]
Sit down, shut up, and listen.
This won't take long.

[Bridge — beat thins to hats and Rhodes; the patter never breaks stride]
The chairman rang at midnight: how did it land, what's the coverage, are we safe —
and I was fluent: the coverage is glowing, the analysts are up late rewriting,
the launch outperformed, the demo held, the room was eating from my hand.
He said where are you now? I said celebrating with the team.
He said you've saved us, you know. I said I know. Get some sleep, Gordon.
Somewhere behind me, an ice bucket. The team was not in the room.

[Out — beat drops to room tone, a notification chime]
By the end of the week the fund had quietly closed out its short.
The note hit at six the next morning — fourteen pages, upgraded us to Buy.
There's a typo in the title. It says "Strong Bye." Nobody's corrected it.
The board extended my contract on the spot. I told the interns the secret:
preparation. Always do the work the night before.

[Final hook — chant, once, dry]
Sit down, shut up, and listen.
This won't take long.
