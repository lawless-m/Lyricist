[Verse 1 — live-room breakbeat, filtered bass, cheap Rhodes stab; voice close-mic'd, dry, fast, grinning]
Ten years on, and it's my bar. I run the optics — both kinds.
Free pour with my back turned and land the measure dead legal:
Weights and Measures came in undercover once. They left a tip.
Tills straight, lines clean, cellar cold, regulars regular —

and when I want the room, I reach for the top shelf. That's all it takes now.
The scaffolders go quiet like it's church. That's a skill, that. That's tenure.
New girl started Monday. Sweet kid. Photogenic with a shaker.
Gary says get her trained up proper. Proper it is, then.

[Hook — all-female gang chant, flat, two lines only]
Whatever she's having —
make it a double.

[Verse 2 — same loop, bass up, hats busier; delivery stays level]
Friday night, the place is heaving, and madam's found her market:
up on the bar for the lads' end, giving them the full performance —
the hair flip, the straw, the body shot she pours at her mate and misses,
the almost-kiss she keeps on almost having, angled at the pool table.
Now, I don't begrudge a girl her theatre. But she's serving watered-down
and calling it top shelf, and on my bar that's a sacking offence.

So when the shift drink hit the dancefloor and the routine came round again,
I called it. Took a handful of arse — both hands, no notice —
and kissed her. French. Centre-floor. Long enough to check her measure.
[the beat cuts for one bar]
The pool table lost its cue ball. Somebody's pint met somebody's phone.
And the act forgot its lines. There's no script for the real thing, is there.

[Hook — chant, unchanged]
Whatever she's having —
make it a double.

[Bridge — beat drops to kick and Rhodes; the patter never wavers]
And here's the boyfriend at the edge of the floor, holding her coat like a question.
I don't miss a beat: you must be Dean. She talks about you constantly.
This? This is training, love. The composure module. All the good houses run it —
they do it at the Savoy. She's got real promise, your girl:
bit green on the mixing, lovely instincts, very coachable.
You should be proud. She's being fast-tracked.
And Dean carried her coat home happy. Man thinks she's management material.

[Out — beat drops to room tone, a glasswasher finishing its cycle]
Monday the rota went up. She's off the lads' Friday. All her shifts are mine now —
her Dean thinks it's for the mentoring.
I tell the trade what I've always told them: pour honest.
Nothing gets sold on my bar that isn't what it says it is.
Gary's handed me the inductions for the whole group now. Every new start.
He says nobody settles them in like I do.

[Final hook — chant, once, dry]
Whatever she's having —
make it a double.
