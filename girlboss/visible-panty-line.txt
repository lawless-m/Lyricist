[Verse 1 — live-room breakbeat, filtered bass, cheap organ stab; voice close-mic'd, dry, fast, pleased with itself]
They audition the weather on smiles. I turned up with a master's:
fronts, occlusions, dew points — I draw my own isobars.
Wardrobe keeps offering blazers; I keep declining the blazers.
The dress is straight-cut, high-necked, navy. Modest as a hymn.

And you can see the line. I know you can see the line.
A visible panty line — visible on purpose. The only thing in this bulletin
that's exactly what it looks like. Call it a public service.
Trust matters in forecasting. I've got nothing to hide. Clearly.

[Hook — all-female gang chant, flat, two lines only]
And now, the weather.
Don't look away.

[Verse 2 — same loop, bass up a notch; delivery stays deadpan]
The map wants reaching: the Faroes are up here, the Scillies are down there,
and the nation checks the temperature in places it could swear it didn't care about.
Camera two is meant to cut wide to the graphic on the turn.
Camera two has developed a loyalty problem. The gallery's gone quiet in my ear.

The sports desk follows me at seven past. Poor Mike.
He gave last night's football to the wrong end of the table,
and read the racing results over a photo of a different horse.
I asked him after if he was feeling alright. He thanked me. For the weather.

[Hook — chant, unchanged]
And now, the weather.
Don't look away.

[Bridge — beat thins to hats and organ; the patter never breaks stride]
The editor called me in: we've had correspondence — forty letters, a petition,
one from a retired colonel, with diagrams. Quite good ones, actually.
I was fluent. I said the dress is a regulation fit, and offered him the receipts.
I said two newsreaders wore this exact cut last month to no correspondence at all.
I said if a line upsets the colonel, wait till he meets the jet stream.
I said — and I believe this — you can't trust a forecast from a woman with something to hide.
He agreed. He didn't notice he agreed. The petition went in the recycling.

[Out — beat drops to room tone, a countdown clock ticking]
We stayed late on Thursday re-recording the Sunday outlook.
It went out identical to the first take. The weather hadn't changed. The log says "technical."
Ratings are up a third since spring — strongest, oddly, in the shipping regions.
They've given me the Christmas special.
Wardrobe's stopped offering the blazers.

[Final hook — chant, once, dry]
And now, the weather.
Don't look away.
