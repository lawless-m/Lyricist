[Verse 1 — live-room breakbeat, filtered bass, cheap organ stab; voice close-mic'd, dry, fast, sly]
Trade fair, hall two, and I've got the end table — best light in the building. I checked.
The list runs six deep. The reserve travels separately. It travels with me.
Blindfold me and pour: I'll name the river, the bank, the slope, near enough the row.
I've spat better wine than most of this hall has sold. Credentials? Framed ones.

Silk blouse, because the halls run warm. Cut low, because silk does that.
A pendant on a long chain — it gives the eye somewhere to start. Wayfinding.
The buyers come to me by eleven; I let the little glasses queue.
And the big one — the one whose pen moves markets — he's booked in at half past two.

[Hook — all-female gang chant, flat, two lines only]

[Verse 2 — same loop, bass up; delivery stays unhurried]
Half past two. He takes the glass like a man on television — all wrist.
First flight, he's writing essays. Second flight, I catch it:
one look, south of the pendant, quick as a shoplifter, while I'm turned to the ice.
He thinks the ice bucket's just an ice bucket. It's silver. It's a mirror with a job.

So the third flight gets the full service. The reserve needs decanting —
it needs air, this one. Let it breathe. Most things improve with a little air —
and a decant done right is done low, and done slow, at the level of the label,
and I read him the back of the bottle from down there like a bedtime story.

Nowhere neutral left to put his eyes. The fire-exit map's getting a second read.
He swallowed the whole third flight. That's not done at a tasting. Everyone spits.
His notes by the end were one word per wine, and the words weren't wine words.
[the beat cuts for one bar]
I stood up. I topped the ice. We discussed logistics.

[Hook — chant, unchanged]
Good body.
Long finish.

[Bridge — beat thins to organ and hats; the patter pours on]
Margaret rang from the vineyard at six — did he come, did he taste, be honest —
and I gave her the day in pouring order: the whites sang, the young red behaved,
the '08 did that thing it does in minute three, and the buyer? Attentive.
Wrote pages early on. Asked after the reserve unprompted.
And the reserve, Margaret? Tasted. Scored. Adored. It wanted air, so I gave it air.
She said you're wasted on wine. I said no such thing. Nothing's wasted on wine.

[Out — beat drops to room tone, a cork easing out]
The order landed Monday: the full list, both vintages, allocation on the reserve.
His published note gave the reserve top of the flight. Says it "opens beautifully."
The reserve never made it to the hall. It spent the fair breathing in my hotel room.
I do every fair for the house now — they ask for me like a vintage.
First rule of the floor, and I teach it: sell at eye level.

[Final hook — chant, once, dry]
Good body.
Long finish.
