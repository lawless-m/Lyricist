[Verse 1 — live-room breakbeat, filtered bass, cheap organ stab; voice close-mic'd, dry, fast, amused with itself]
Flagship branch, ladies' footwear. I run this floor like a ward sister:
every size, every width, the patents under glass like they're evidence.
I can read a foot through the sock. I know a five when she's swearing she's a four.
I fit kneeling — it's the proper way — and the husbands on the bench study the ceiling.

Then Germaine wrote the column. Our window in the photograph, uncredited.
Four inches of contempt, she called them. A pantomime for men. Degrading.
I cut it out and laminated it. It lives by the till now, like scripture.
Best advert the brand never paid for. I bought my pair the same lunchtime. Red.

[Hook — all-female gang chant, flat, two lines only]
Germaine says these are fuck-me shoes.
That's why I bought them.

[Verse 2 — same loop, bass up a notch; delivery stays deadpan]
Quarterly stock audit: head office send their area man down to count us.
Early train, scanner, lanyard, a printed plan that had him back for the fast one home.
I said you can't rush a proper count. I wore the red ones. Well —
you dress for stocktake same as anything else. Presentation matters in retail.

He counted the display wall and arrived at a new number every pass.
He scanned the same box till it beeped at him out of pity. He apologised to a shelf.
I steadied the ladder while he wobbled on the facts up by the party heels,
and at the stockroom — no windows, one door — I shut it. For the acoustics.
I said: you've seen the column by the till. They're called what they're called, love.
I don't argue with the literature.
[the beat cuts for one bar]
Stocktake resumed at three. The afternoon went quicker. He'd lost his appetite for spot checks.

[Hook — chant, unchanged]
Germaine says these are fuck-me shoes.
That's why I bought them.

[Bridge — beat strips to hats and organ; the patter doesn't blink]
Denise rang down near closing — how's it going, is he finding anything —
and I gave her chapter and verse: Denise, the man is thoroughness itself.
He's insisted on doing ladies' personally. Box by box. Very hands-on with the fives.
We've done the wall, we've done the annex, we've been at it in the stockroom since three.
I said tell head office this branch has nothing whatsoever to hide.
I said audits like this are why I love this company, honestly.
She said what would we do without you. I let the question answer itself.

[Out — beat drops to room tone, a till drawer rolling open]
Sign-off: best count in the region. No discrepancies. A commendation for Denise's displays.
There's a day-return to the city in the till drawer, dated audit day. Unused. Nobody's come for it.
I tell the Saturday girls: do your reading. Listen to the serious women.
Germaine hasn't steered me wrong yet.
And head office? They've put us down as his personal visit now. Quarterly. In writing. We're honoured.

[Final hook — chant, once, dry]
Germaine says these are fuck-me shoes.
That's why I bought them.
