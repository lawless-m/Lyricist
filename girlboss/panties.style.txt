Late-90s / early-00s indie hip-hop. Live-sounding breakbeat, filtered bass, cheap organ or Rhodes stab. Female vocal, conversational-to-fast, close-mic'd, dry — the voice sits in front of the beat, not in it. Hook can be all-female-gang-chanted or doubled. No sung chorus. Nothing polished; the record should sound like it cost very little and knew exactly what it was doing.
