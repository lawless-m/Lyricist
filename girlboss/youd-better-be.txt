[Verse 1 — live-room breakbeat, filtered bass, cheap organ stab; voice close-mic'd, dry, fast, pleased with itself]
First week, new floor — I learned every name by Wednesday:
who does what, who thinks they do, who signs.
They gave me the desk by the window like it was nothing.
I'd asked for it. Sightlines matter in an open plan.

And I'm good at this — inbox zero, minutes typed up same day,
the printer likes me, and the printer likes nobody.
The head of the whole division sits in a corner office, all glass.
Which means, if you think about it, he works in a display case.

[Hook — all-female gang chant, flat, two lines only]
Are you looking up my skirt?
You'd better be.

[Verse 2 — same loop, bass up a notch; delivery stays deadpan]
The hem is regulation — I measured: two fingers above the knee,
compliant from every angle anyone would admit to using.
I take the stairs up, never down. You learn a lot about a man
from where he stands in an atrium when he thinks he's reading his phone.

Tuesday he missed his floor in the lift and rode it to the roof.
He signed the quarterly forecast "kind regards." It went to print.
I asked to borrow a pen; he handed me three, and one was engraved.
I kept that one. He hasn't asked for it back. He won't.

[Hook — chant, unchanged]
Are you looking up my skirt?
You'd better be.

[Bridge — beat thins to hats and organ; the patter never breaks stride]
HR invited me in Thursday for a word about "attention on the floor" —
someone had raised the division head's... focus, as a concern.
I was magnificent. I said focus? The man is a machine.
I said those Q3 numbers don't audit themselves, I said honestly,
some people in this building would see filth in a fire drill.
And I recrossed my legs, and the woman from HR agreed with me,
and we both said "standards," and I took the stairs back up. Slowly.

[Out — beat drops to room tone, a lift bell]
Friday the lift stuck for half an hour between the fourth floor and the fifth.
Facilities looked it over and found nothing wrong at all.
Monday they made me permanent — first in my intake, on a word from upstairs.
I take the stairs now. Better for everyone's health.

[Final hook — chant, once, dry]
Are you looking up my skirt?
You'd better be.
