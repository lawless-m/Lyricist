[Verse 1 — live-room breakbeat, filtered bass, wah guitar stabs; fast, dry, conversational]
Four horses on the yard, three of them mine, and the fourth's a loan I ride for a friend in Kent.
Lorry loaded by seven on a Sunday, home with the rosettes before the pub's got going.
I do my own plaiting, my own entries, my own feet — I've never paid a groom in my life.
Well. I've never paid a groom.
Immy's nineteen. Deferred her place, drives herself over in a Corsa with a foot of hay in the footwell,
turned up in March and stood at my stable door for an hour like she was waiting to be let in on something.
So I let her hold the grey. That's all it takes, if you've picked the right one.
Second week she was doing my haynets. Third week she was doing them better than me.

[Hook — all-female gang chant, flat, two lines only]
She mucks out four. I ride one.
Best deal on the yard.

[Verse 2 — same loop, bass up a notch; delivery stays deadpan]
Here's the standing arrangement, and I have never once asked her for any of it:
half six on a weekday, both rugs changed, all four skipped out, water carried through the ice
with her sleeves pulled over her hands because the good gloves live in the tack room in case they get ruined.
Poo-picks the paddock. Sweeps the barn. Cleans my bridle till the browband comes up wet.
And here's the wage: I tell her she's got an eye. I tell her the grey goes better for her than he does for me.
Twenty minutes on the old boy Thursday if the light holds and I'm not using the school.
She writes it in a diary. I've seen the diary. She'd do the whole week again for the Thursday.
I'm bringing her on. That's what you say about a young horse. It's what I say about her.

[Hook — chant, unchanged]
She mucks out four. I ride one.
Best deal on the yard.

[Bridge — beat thins to hats and one wah stab; the patter never breaks stride]
Her mum rang Sunday. Very polite. Asked was there any chance of it turning into a job.
And I was fluent. I said Denise, what she's getting here you genuinely cannot buy —
I said she's on my lorry every weekend, she's got her hands on four horses a day,
I said there are working pupils in Newmarket paying for this, paying, and not seeing half what she sees.
I said I'm putting her in for her Stage Two in the spring. I said she'll learn more off me in a month than a year of lessons.
I said she's doing me a favour, honestly, it keeps her out of trouble.
Then I wrote Monday's list on the whiteboard while Denise was still thanking me.
Seven jobs on it. She'd done eleven by the Wednesday.

[Out — beat drops to room tone, a kicked bucket, a radio in the barn]
She turned down the garden centre. Twelve pound an hour, Saturdays, her mate had got her the interview.
Said she couldn't, she's needed here.
Marion knocked two months off my livery for taking the girl under my wing.
You cannot buy that kind of work ethic. You have to give them something to look up to.
She's asked if she can start doing Sundays as well.

[Final hook — chant, once, dry]
She mucks out four. I ride one.
Best deal on the yard.
