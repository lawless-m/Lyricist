[Verse 1 — spoken, fast, conversational, dry, flat, no melody]
Three o'clock start, and I'm the only light on in the row.
Sixty loaves, forty baps, tin stuff on the left, the sourdough gets the good table.
Pitch fee's twenty-two quid, cash, in Derek's hand before he's got his coat off.
I bring my own gazebo, my own scales, my own float, my own gas.
The eggs are on my left. The cheese is on my right. That's the whole of my world till one.
I've got a card up that says ASK ME WHAT'S IN IT, and I love it when they do.
Everything on this stall came out of my hands before it got light.
I am the reason this end of the market smells like anything at all.

[Hook — flat gang chant, shouted not sung, two lines]
Would you like to see my baps?
Freshly baked.

[Verse 2 — spoken, faster, dry, flat, no melody]
The cheese lad's twenty-six and he hasn't turned a truckle since half eight.
His brie's gone to liquid in the sun and he is not thinking about his brie.
I knock the dough back at the front of the stall where they can all see it.
It takes the arm, that. It takes both. I do it slowly, and I do it for a long time.
Then I let it prove. You cannot rush a prove — the dough knows, and it tells on you later.
He's answered a question about the chutney with the price of a mature cheddar.
So when he came over for a bap for his break — his break was at seven — I said:
one o'clock, everyone packs down, my gazebo is the last one standing, and I'll want both hands.
And then a woman wanted a bloomer, so I did that.

[Hook — flat gang chant, shouted not sung, two lines]
Would you like to see my baps?
Freshly baked.

[Bridge — rapid spoken count, no melody, breakbeat only]
Market manager rings at half eleven. Derek. About the pitch, the licence, the bins.
And I am marvellous with Derek. I don't answer Derek, I solve Derek.
I tell him the drain by stall four is backing up and I've bagged it myself with hessian,
I tell him the egg girl's canopy is a pole short and she'll take the corner if he offers it,
I tell him yes to the Christmas one, both weekends, and I'll bring my own lights.
And in the middle of that, with Derek still on, a gentleman comes to the counter,
so I say it into the phone and to him at the same time, and I don't cover the mouthpiece:
would you like to see my baps? Freshly baked.
Derek said sorry — was that meant for him. I said no, Derek. That one wasn't for you.

[Out — spoken, flat, then stop]
Sunday's dough went to prove before pack-down and it didn't get knocked back till gone three.
Came out flat. Over-proved, an hour past it, easy. Yeast doesn't care what you tell it.
I told the girl on the eggs: never mark down before one, they'll only wait you out.
Give them the price, give them the smile, give them absolutely nothing else for free.
They've moved me to the top of the row now, by the gate.
Everybody has to walk past me on the way in.
