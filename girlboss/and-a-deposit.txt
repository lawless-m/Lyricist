[Verse 1 — spoken, fast, conversational, dry, flat, no melody]
Established nineteen sixty-three, and the carpet in reception has not been changed since.
We do not say dating in this building. We say introductions. We say courtship.
Six-month membership, twelve hundred, and the interview is two hours and it's with me.
I take a history. Schooling, war record if there is one, mother, and what he reads.
Then I write the profile myself, longhand, and every word of it is flattering and true.
Four gentlemen on my books this quarter and I could tell you all their shoe sizes.
The service is rehearsal. That is the actual product. They come here to practise.
And I play her. Whoever she is. That's what the twelve hundred is for.

[Bridge — rapid spoken count, no melody, breakbeat only]
Mrs Alderton rings from upstairs. Founder. Eighty-one. Rings about renewals on a Friday.
And I get in first, because you always get in first with Mrs Alderton:
I said I have a concern. I said I'm not sure he's ready to be introduced to anybody yet.
I said I would hate to put a gentleman in front of a lady before he can hold a room.
And she spent eleven minutes telling me I am the most conscientious girl she has ever had,
that the others rush them, that I'm the only one who understands what the agency is for.
I said that's very kind. I logged the call. Fifteen minutes. It goes on his account.

Nobody comes here to meet anybody. They come here to practise being wanted.
