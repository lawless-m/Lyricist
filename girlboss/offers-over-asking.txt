[Verse 1 — live-room breakbeat, filtered bass, cheap Rhodes stab; voice close-mic'd, dry, fast, polished]
My face is on the boards this side of the park. My patch. My records.
I can quote you what your neighbour got, to the pound — and what he tells the street he got.
Chain-free, south-facing, five minutes from the good school if you jog. I wrote the listing.
Open house, one till four. The vendors are at his mother's. Their keys answer to me.

Staging: lamps on at noon, lilies in the hall, a loaf timed for half past one,
coffee beans in a warm pan — nobody has ever lived like this, and everybody wants to.
I opened the curtains in the master myself. The light in there, one till three? Forgiving.
And the serious ones come early. The serious ones always come early.

[Hook — all-female gang chant, flat, two lines only]
Offers over asking.
No chain.

[Verse 2 — same loop, bass up; delivery stays courteous]
Ten past one: cash buyer, proof of funds, recently unattached. Says he's "just looking."
They all say just looking. I said take your time — I do the showings personally.
Kitchen: he tested a tap like they do, found the sprayer, wore half of it home on his cuff.
Lounge: he aimed the laser measure backwards and read out his own height.

I walked him through the garden slow — west-facing, you get the whole evening — then up.
The master shows best in the afternoon. I let the room do its work. I helped the room.
I said: storage runs the full wall. I said: it's a lot of house for one man.
Then I drew the curtains — that sun will bleach a carpet, and this one's included.
[the beat cuts for one bar]
We came down at two. The loaf was done. He asked what the vendors were hoping for,
and his pen found paper before I'd finished saying the number.

[Hook — chant, unchanged]
Offers over asking.
No chain.

[Bridge — beat softens to Rhodes and hats; the patter stays in brochure voice]
The vendor rang at three from his mother's — how's the turnout, did they clock the boiler —
and I'm smooth as fresh paint: tremendous interest, darling, twelve through the door,
a bidding war brewing, and one gentleman — cash — who's fallen for the place completely.
Spent a good while in the master. Very taken with the storage. Didn't want to leave.
The boiler? Nobody so much as opened the cupboard.
They were looking at the light.

[Out — beat drops to room tone, a SOLD slat clacking onto a board]
The offer landed before the loaf had cooled: over asking, subject to nothing.
He waived the survey. Said he'd seen everything he needed to see.
The vendors cried. The street has a new record — ask what it went for, I'll round down.
And I've taken on his old flat too. Both ends of the chain, one agent. Tidy.
Full disclosure, that's my code: every feature shown, no hidden defects.
The developer's given me the show homes off the back of it. Seven days a week.
Somebody has to do the showings.

[Final hook — chant, once, dry]
Offers over asking.
No chain.
