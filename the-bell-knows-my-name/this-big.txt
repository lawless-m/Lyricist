[Verse 1 — slow, intimate, solo violin, rubato]
She got one on the second bend, and she held it up to show
and it sat in her two hands with an inch to spare each side
I said there used to be bigger, I said it like a gift
and she looked at the water like she was the one who'd let me down

[Pre-chorus — building, accordion swells in]
and the fiddle tunes down a little
and tells us it's the same tune

[Chorus — building, full band]
so cast it long, cast it hopeless, let the current have the day
his were bigger than mine, and mine are bigger than hers
we hand it on a little worse and call it handing on
Vesna, will you tell them it was better?

[Verse 2 — slow again]
Marko sold the boat in the spring and he works the road now
he waves from the cab when he passes, he does not look down at the bank
his father had the licence and his father had it too
and the river is still there, and it is still called the same

[Bridge — half-time, sparse, then huge]
maybe it was never generous
maybe there were only fewer of us
maybe there is no year to go back to
that anybody actually saw
— (violin builds, tempo doubles) —
so let it ruuun—

[Final Chorus — biggest, gang vocals on the last line]
cast it long, cast it hopeless, let the evening take the light
his were bigger than mine, and mine are bigger than hers
I have never lied about it and I have never told it straight
THEY WERE THIS BIG
