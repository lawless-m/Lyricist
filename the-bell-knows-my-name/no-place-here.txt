[Verse 1 — slow, intimate, solo violin, rubato]
He came up the lane in a car that was wrong for the road
with a case full of wire and a book where he wrote what he was told
I was seventeen and certain and I got there before my father did
and I said it loud enough that all of them could hear

[Pre-chorus — building, accordion swells in]
and the fiddle keeps no copies
of the things it used to know

[Chorus — fast, full band]
so play it old, play it useless, let the valley have it back
we told him that his machines had no business in this place
we saved it from him, and we did not save it
did you get any of it down, before we turned you out?

[Verse 2 — slow again]
Anton sits in the chair by the door with his hands in his lap
he can hear it well enough, he just can't lift them to the strings
I have carried a phone in my pocket for years and years
and I have never asked him to play

[Bridge — half-time, sparse, then huge]
maybe it was never ours to keep
maybe it was only ever lent
maybe a thing you can't make a copy of
is a thing that was always going to go
— (violin builds, tempo doubles) —
so sing it slooow—

[Final Chorus — biggest, gang vocals on the last line]
play it old, play it useless, let the neighbours have it back
we saved it from him, and we did not save it
I never asked his name, and he wrote mine in the book
YOUR COMPUTERS HAVE NO PLACE HERE
