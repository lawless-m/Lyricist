[Verse 1 — slow, intimate, solo violin, rubato]
The wood is not in and it is the end of October
she has had it in by the first week for as long as I have known the house
I did not ask about it, I said something about the weather turning
and she agreed with me about the weather, and we stood there a while

[Pre-chorus — building, accordion swells in]
and the fiddle will not hold its tuning
in a room this cold

[Chorus — building, full band]
so bank it up, keep it lit, get the door shut on the dark
winter will end, and that is the smallest true thing that I own
I have said it to somebody every year of my life
Lena, shall I bring the wood in?

[Verse 2 — slow again]
There is a stack under the tarp from last year and it is dry enough
it would take me an afternoon and I have had a great many afternoons
her coat is on the hook and it has been on the hook since the Sunday
and I have stood in that kitchen and offered her a fact

[Bridge — half-time, sparse, then huge]
maybe it was never about getting through it
maybe getting through it is the whole of it
maybe I say it because it is the one thing I am sure of
and being sure of a thing is not the same as helping
— (violin builds, tempo doubles) —
so bank it uuup—

[Final Chorus — biggest, gang vocals on the last line]
bank it up, keep it lit, get the door shut on the dark
winter will end, and that is the smallest true thing that I own
I have said it to somebody every year of my life
Lena, shall I bring the wood in?
WINTER WILL END
