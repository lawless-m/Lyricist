[Verse 1 — slow, intimate, solo violin, rubato]
I have the letter in my coat and I have had it since the Thursday
I put my boots on and I said the weather looked all right
he got his stick and he came out and he did not ask me anything
and the lane was doing what the lane does in the light

[Pre-chorus — building, accordion swells in]
and the fiddle will not finish
the bar it is already in

[Chorus — building, full band]
so hold it here, hold it open, let the afternoon go slack
tomorrow is already true, it is only not here yet
I will say it in the morning and I am not saying it now
Nicu, shall we go the long way back?

[Verse 2 — slow again]
My father walked me up to the top field the day before he sold it
he talked about the fencing and he never said a word
I was nineteen and I thought he wanted company
and I have had thirty years to work out what that was

[Bridge — half-time, sparse, then huge]
maybe tomorrow was always the worst day
maybe that is the whole of what it is for
maybe today is the only place that anybody has ever stood
and we keep on being asked to leave it
— (violin builds, tempo doubles) —
so walk it slooow—

[Final Chorus — biggest, gang vocals on the last line]
hold it here, hold it open, let the light go off the hill
tomorrow is already true, it is only not here yet
I will say it in the morning and I am not saying it now
Nicu, shall we go the long way back?
WE ARE STILL IN TODAY
