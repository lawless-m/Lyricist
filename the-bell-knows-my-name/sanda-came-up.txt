[Verse 1 — slow, intimate, solo violin, rubato]
They've got it going on the wall by the pump at half past eight
two of them turning the rope and the rest of them waiting their turn
they've got the tune near enough and they've got the words all wrong
and they've made it fast, and they've made it a game

[Pre-chorus — building, accordion swells in]
and the fiddle takes their tempo
and forgets the way it went

[Chorus — building, full band]
so sing it wrong, sing it louder, let it carry down the lane
I could give them the right words in a minute, and I would rather hear this
it was a lament when it came to me and it is a skipping rhyme now
Ilka, shall I leave it as it is?

[Verse 2 — slow again]
My aunt sat me on this wall and made me sing it back to her
she said it isn't a song, it's a fence, and you stay the near side of it
there was a girl, and the weir, and a night in a bad year
and they sing it at the weir now, and they sing it going in

[Bridge — half-time, sparse, then huge]
maybe a song is only what it does
maybe it was never about her at all
maybe the ones that last
are the ones that let go of the reason
— (violin builds, tempo doubles) —
so let it gooo—

[Final Chorus — biggest, gang vocals on the last line]
sing it wrong, sing it louder, let the whole street have it back
I could give them the right words in a minute, and I would rather hear this
the water took her in the version that I know
SANDA WENT DOWN, SANDA CAME UP
