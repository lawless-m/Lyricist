[Verse 1 — slow, intimate, solo violin, rubato]
The wall by the pump is warm at eight and there is nobody on it
the rope is in the shed where somebody coiled it properly
I told mine there was nothing here and I meant it as a kindness
and I was right, and it worked, and this is what it sounds like

[Pre-chorus — building, accordion swells in]
and the fiddle plays both parts now
and nobody hears the join

[Chorus — building, full band]
so play it thin, play it early, there is no one to keep awake
I did not lose them. I sent them.
we did the good thing all together and we did it one by one
Petru, do they have a wall like this?

[Verse 2 — slow again]
Lidia kept the school going for two terms with a class of one
she marked his book at her own table with the heating off
he is an engineer in a city with a river I have not seen
and she still has the register, and there is one name in it

[Bridge — half-time, sparse, then huge]
maybe a village is not a thing that keeps
maybe it was always just a place that people are from
maybe the ones who stayed
are only the ones who couldn't
— (violin builds, tempo doubles) —
so send them oooon—

[Final Chorus — biggest, gang vocals on the last line]
play it thin, play it early, let the swallows have the eaves
I did not lose them. I sent them.
and every one of them stayed gone, and every one of them was right
THEY ALL GOT OUT
