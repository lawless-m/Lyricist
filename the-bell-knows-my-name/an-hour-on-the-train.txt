[Verse 1 — slow, intimate, solo violin, rubato]
There was a marquee on a lawn that somebody was paid to mow
and a man behind a list at the door telling all of us where to go
I left the fiddle in the car. I never carried it in.
I said she wouldn't want the noise — and that is where it began

[Pre-chorus — building, accordion swells in]
and the fiddle learns to whisper
the way they do indoors

[Chorus — fast, full band]
so play it clean, play it careful, like the rooms they keep in town
we say that she is lost to us, we say it like a fact
it's an hour on the train, and I have made it a border
Zora, does he know you can sing?

[Verse 2 — slow again]
She could back a trailer blind, she could stand a nervous horse
she had a laugh you heard from the bottom of the lane
now there's a card at Christmas in a hand I do not know
and I have not written back, and I have not picked up the phone

[Bridge — half-time, sparse, then huge]
maybe she was never lost
maybe we just stopped the drive
maybe a road goes both directions
and we only ever used the one
— (violin builds, tempo doubles) —
so ride it hooome—

[Final Chorus — biggest, gang vocals on the last line]
play it clean, play it careful, let them hear it through the wall
we said that she was lost to us, we said it for ten years
it's an hour on the train, and I have made it a border
Zora, does he know you can sing?
and not one of us has gone
