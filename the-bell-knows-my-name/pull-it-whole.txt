[Verse 1 — slow, intimate, solo violin, rubato]
It came up the water the way everything comes up the water, from below
first the far side, then the bend, then the bit we cut the hay on
it does not fight you, it just arrives, and it arrives again
and there is nothing on this bank that grows as fast as that

[Pre-chorus — building, accordion swells in]
and the fiddle plays what it was given
and never asked where it came from

[Chorus — building, full band]
so pull it slow, pull it whole, get the white root out of the wet
it is not a war and we are not losing it, it is a Sunday and we are late
by the winter it is gone and the bank goes with it
Dana, will you bring yours down next year?

[Verse 2 — slow again]
Sava came down every spring for as long as I have been coming
he had the good gloves and he never once complained about the gloves
his boy is in the town and the town is a good hour
and none of us rang him, and the far side is all of it now

[Bridge — half-time, sparse, then huge]
maybe there was never a year we were winning
maybe holding is the whole of the job
maybe a thing you defend every spring for a hundred springs
is not a place any more, it is a habit
— (violin builds, tempo doubles) —
so pull it slooow—

[Final Chorus — biggest, gang vocals on the last line]
pull it slow, pull it whole, get the white root out of the wet
it is not a war and we are not losing it, it is a Sunday and we are late
we will be down here again and the spring will not care
Dana, will you bring yours down next year?
NOT THIS BANK
